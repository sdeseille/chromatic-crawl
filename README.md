# Chromatic Crawl

![thumbnail](thumbnail_img.png)

## Description

The Moon Folk struck during the last eclipse, stealing every pot of gold from the Leprechaun's hoard. Without them, the rainbow has faded, and the world has drained to shades of gray.

You are a unicorn, and you must descend into the Moon Folk's dungeon to reclaim what was stolen.

Explore room by room, dash down the creatures guarding each door, and face the color guardian waiting at the end of every level. Each guardian you defeat gives back one color of the rainbow — and with it, a new power for your unicorn, letting you overcome obstacles and guardians that stood in your way before.

Restore all seven colors, rebuild the rainbow, and bring the world back to life.

## How to Play

* **Arrow keys** — move your unicorn around the room.
* **Space** — Dash. This is your unicorn's only attack: a short burst in the direction you're moving (or the last direction you moved, if you're standing still). Dash through an enemy or guardian to defeat it — walking into one just hurts you instead, so timing your dash is everything.

A room stays locked (red doors) until every enemy inside it is defeated, then its doors turn green and open the way forward. The last room of each level holds a color guardian: watch for its telegraph flash before it charges, dodge or dash through the charge, and punish it while it recovers.

## Colors & Blessings

Every guardian you defeat restores one color to the world — and grants a permanent power to match:

| Color | Guardian falls, you gain... |
|---|---|
| 🔴 Red | **Vitality** — one extra heart of maximum health |
| 🟠 Orange | **Ember Dash** — your dash covers more ground |
| 🟡 Yellow | **Solar Haste** — your dash recovers faster |
| 🟢 Green | **Thorn Ward** — a banked shield charge that blocks your next hit for free |
| 🔵 Blue | **Tidal Grace** — longer invincibility after taking a hit |
| 🟣 Indigo | **Arcane Echo** — extended dash reach, so a slightly short dash still connects |
| 🟪 Violet | **Chromatic Overdrive** — the rainbow is complete, with a bonus for restoring the final color |

Each color pays off a different way — offense, defense, or recovery — so restoring the rainbow keeps changing how you play, right up to the last guardian.

## Difficulty Levels

* Only one level of difficulty.

Clear every room, defeat every guardian, and see the rainbow restored!
